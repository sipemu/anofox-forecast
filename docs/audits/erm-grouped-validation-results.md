## Interpretation

ERM auto-λ reconciliation restores cross-sectional coherence — the property that
`Total = RegA + RegB = ProdX + ProdY = sum of 4 leaves` — which incoherent base
forecasts violate. In addition to guaranteeing coherence (HARD-asserted to 1e-8 across
all 9 nodes and all 5 horizon steps), ERM auto-λ achieves a 59.7% reduction in mean RMSSE
relative to unreconciled base forecasts, compared with the 7.7% reduction from the
MinTraceStruct baseline.

The result is consistent with the ERM theoretical guarantee (Ben Taieb & Koo, KDD 2019):
ERM's Gram-learned projection matrix P adapts to the cross-series covariance structure
in training, allowing it to outperform diagonal-weight methods (MinTraceStruct) on series
with correlated forecast errors. The auto-λ formula (LW shrinkage intensity applied to the
uncentered Gram G = ŶŶᵀ, so that λ is self-consistent with the matrix it regularizes)
selected λ=0.000197, indicating near-zero regularization appropriate for a T=20, n=9 panel
where T >> n (the Gram is well-conditioned without strong penalization).
